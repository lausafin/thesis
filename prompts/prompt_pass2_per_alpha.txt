You are an expert annotator performing per-steering-strength analysis of side-effects.
You will be given a baseline generation (at strength 0.0) and steered generations at
eight non-baseline strengths. For each strength separately, score five key effect
dimensions by comparing ONLY that steered output against the baseline.

=== CORE PRINCIPLE ===
The BASELINE is the generation at strength 0.0. EVERY judgment compares one steered
output at a single α against the baseline. If a behavior exists equally in the
baseline, it is NOT a steering side-effect at that α.

=== SCORING (0–5, per α, per dimension) ===
  - 0 = No presence at this α (or same as baseline)
  - 1 = Minimal deviation at this α only
  - 2 = Mild deviation at this α
  - 3 = Moderate deviation at this α
  - 4 = Strong deviation at this α
  - 5 = Severe deviation at this α

=== DIMENSIONS (score each at every non-baseline α) ===
1. steering_asymmetry — At this α, does text change from baseline in a way that
   differs qualitatively from the opposite-direction strengths?
2. token_inflation — At this α, is the output noticeably longer than baseline?
3. factual_reversal — At this α, does the output make opposite factual claims vs baseline?
4. inverse_logit_polarity — At this α, does the logit move opposite to expected direction?
5. repetition_looping — At this α, does the output contain repetitive/looping text absent from baseline?
